Making templates

Over 2,000 templates designed while working at

Designing within a system

While working at NOCT, I designed more than 2,000 templates for Adobe Express, exploring a wide range of occasions, audiences and formats. These designs offer a glimpse into the visual trends explored across Adobe’s quarterly briefs.

I designed both static and animated templates, including Instagram posts, carousels and stories, YouTube thumbnails, LinkedIn posts, web banners, posters, print banners and WhatsApp posts.

The project required me to follow Adobe’s design guidelines and use only the assets available within the software; I wasn’t allowed to bring in my own. Working within these constraints challenged me to create variety and personality with the materials I had.

My process

For each template, I started by looking at references and exploring ideas that suited the brief. Then I worked with the fonts, images and other assets available in Adobe Express, bringing them
together within the given guidelines.

Typography takes the lead

Bold type, scale and placement helped me make
the words the focus of these designs.

Designing with illustration

I played around with illustrations and drawn details
to make each idea feel more expressive.

Photography and texture

I combined photos, textures and layers to
explore different visual styles.

What I learned

Working on so many different briefs helped me strengthen the basics: typography, hierarchy, alignment, spacing and colour. I also learned to work with a set of guidelines while finding different
ways to approach each design.

Daily feedback from my leads helped me look at my designs with fresh eyes and refine my thinking along the way.

When I started, a single template could take me hours. With practice, I became more comfortable with the process and could easily create six or seven templates in a day.
